04 / Audio Works

NERX修复企划 NERX Repair Plan

Logic Pro X // Dolby Atmos // 2025

背景: Background: 在当下的响度战争中，音乐的起伏被无情压缩。我们听到的往往是“响亮”的波形，而非“呼吸”的音乐。 In the current loudness war, the dynamics of music are ruthlessly compressed. What we hear is often a 'loud' waveform, not 'breathing' music.

目标: Objective: 利用杜比全景声（Dolby Atmos）的高宽容度，解构原有分轨，重建动态余量。 Leveraging the high headroom of Dolby Atmos to deconstruct original stems and rebuild dynamic range.

监听: Monitoring: Logic Pro -> AirPods Pro 2 (Head Tracking On).
确保在消费级设备上也能传递出录音棚级别的临场感。 Ensuring studio-level presence is delivered even on consumer devices.
